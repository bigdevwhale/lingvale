# Privacy

Lingvale has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't ask for internet access.

It reads, on the phone, what the other -ling apps installed on the same phone show it through their read-only bridge: their pets, the kinds of pets, which days something was done and the last week's events (for Pennyling, the category and the amount as Pennyling writes it). It keeps the last answer of each app in its own private storage so the village opens quickly. It changes nothing in those apps and sends nothing anywhere.

Uninstalling Lingvale deletes everything it kept.
