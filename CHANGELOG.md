## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-16

- **Houses spread over the whole valley:** every number of houses has its own layout around the path and the stream, so a small village no longer huddles at the top and leaves the bottom empty. Fewer houses stand a little bigger, and decorations step aside to the nearest free spot when a house takes theirs.
- **Achievements:** 79 of them in five sections – the village, things done, steadiness, residents and special occasions (an early bird, a night owl, a full week, a comeback and more). A trophy button in the header shows how many are waiting; each one brings fireflies, and once reached it stays.
- **Neighbours talk:** residents now chat about the house next door – an egg about to hatch, a master to learn from, a busy day.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-16

The first release of Lingvale, the village of the pets from all our apps.

- **The valley:** a pixel map where you build a house for each app you use – the tea house (Puffling), the basement gym (Pumpling), the burrow (Pennyling), the cinema (Reelkin), the academy tower (Inkling), the tavern (Questling), the studio (Songling), the writing desk (Keyling) and the reading room (Familiar). The team walks in front of its house; lit windows at night, a fair with flags when three houses are busy on one day.
- **Fireflies, the shop and levels:** everything done in the apps brings fireflies, more when several houses are busy on the same day; 16 decorations for the valley, some with something extra (the notice board's news, the post box's weekly letter, the campfire where residents gather in the evening); the village grows from a hamlet to a small town.
- **Weekly tasks** made of what the apps did, and residents who talk about their days.
- **Inside a house:** the app's own room with its pets, the week in wax seals, the week and month against the ones before, streaks, the time of day, every resident and the house's book.
- **The stamp album** of every creature of every house, and **Days:** the day hour by hour, the week by house, which houses go together, a village gazette and a patchwork quilt of the year.
- Reads the apps through their read-only bridge (protocol v1); no internet permission, nothing leaves the phone. Russian and English, light and dark.
